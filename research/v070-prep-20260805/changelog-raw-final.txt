- resumable per-crate publish — the v0.69.0 first publish shipped 5/9 crates then 429'd on crates.io's new-crate burst limit, and the all-or-nothing 'cargo publish --workspace' rerun hard-failed on 'already exists' instead of resuming. Publish now goes per-crate in dependency order, skips versions already live (registry API), waits out 429s (620s, 6 attempts), and workflow_dispatch gains a publish=true recovery door.
