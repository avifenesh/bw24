CORRECTION TO PLAN.md §3.4 — the "256K does not fit" constraint was priced for a
DIFFERENT BOX, and does not transfer to the PP-2 pair.
=====================================================================================

PLAN.md §3.4 asks "Fits official-IQ4_XS headroom (26.8 GiB)?" — that 26.8 GiB is from
PLAN.md:161, "Both fit 4x 5090 (128 GiB): official + MTP Q8_0 = 101.2 GiB -> 26.8 GiB
headroom". Phase 1 was written against the sku-repick premise of a 4x RTX 5090 (4 x 32
GiB = 128 GiB) box (PLAN.md:5). The owner's 2026-08-06 doctrine call moved the SKU to
2x RTX PRO 6000 Server 96GB = 191.19 GiB measured. Headroom after weights + MTP is
89.95 GiB, not 26.8 GiB — 3.4x more.

Two of §3.4's three inputs also do not match the code:

  (a) It prices FP16 and FP8 KV. memra's DEFAULT with no env set is q8_0 K / q5_1 V
      (kv_blk_bytes(), memra-kv/src/lib.rs:37-42) = 1856 B/tok/layer, which is 45%
      of the FP16 row it calls "22.5 GiB at 128K". The real default-format number is
      10.20 GiB at 128K and 20.39 GiB at 256K.
  (b) The 26.8 GiB comparison is against a whole-box aggregate, but KV is allocated
      per-STAGE by the device that owns the layer (memra-kv/src/lib.rs:253), so the
      binding constraint is per-card, not pair-aggregate.

Redone against this box, at the pp.rs default even cut (23/22 layers):

  256K, memra default q8_0/q5_1: KV = 10.42 GiB on stage 0, 9.97 GiB on stage 1.
  Even with weights split evenly (~48.9 GiB/stage), stage 0 lands ~59.3 of 95.59 GiB.

So on THIS box, 256K fits the allocator as it stands, and work item (F) (the SWA ring
buffer) is a memory OPTIMIZATION here rather than the precondition it was on 4x32 GiB.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH — three things, none of them measured yet:

  1. Activation and graph-pool footprint at 256K is unknown. PLAN.md:187 already flags
     this ("measured there, no number claimed here") and that still holds. At 45 layers
     x 96 SWA heads the prefill activation peak is not a rounding error, and the 93.5:1
     prefill-heavy traffic profile means long prefills are the common case, not the tail.
  2. The even layer-count split is not the even BYTE split. 3 dense + 42 MoE means the
     first stage's layers are cheaper than the second's, so the real per-stage weight
     bytes need the loader's tensor->layer map. That is a first-boot measurement.
  3. Whether 256K is CORRECT at 256K (rope/mask/SWA behavior that far out) is an
     exactness question this arithmetic says nothing about.

Therefore the honest listing context stays 128K for now: it is the number this lane can
defend, and 256K becomes a claim only after a measured boot at 256K plus the exactness
battery at depth. What changed is the REASON for the cap — it is no longer "the
allocator cannot express it".

q27 CO-RESIDENCE (the owner's open question):
  Step weights 97.78 + Step MTP 3.45 + q27 14.63 = 115.86 GiB of the 191.19 GiB pair,
  leaving 75.32 GiB for KV + activations for BOTH models. Step's own 256K KV at the
