READ:
  * every instantiation emits exactly 128 MMAs -> identical MMA COUNT, identical tiling.
  * S32 arms emit IMMA.16832.S8.S8; F32 arms emit QMMA.16832.F32.E4M3.E4M3.
  * S32 pays 512 extra I2FP int->float converts (inherent to accumulating in s32 and
    folding into an f32 sum). Those converts are charged to the S32 arm, i.e. the
    instrument is GENEROUS to F32.
  * S32 still wins ~20% while executing MORE instructions => the delta is MMA
    throughput of the accumulator class, not instruction count or memory traffic.
