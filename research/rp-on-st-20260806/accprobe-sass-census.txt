        QMMA.SF.16832.F32.E4M3.E4M3.E8   (block_scale, .SF = scale factor, .E8 = ue8m0)
        QMMA.16832.F32.E4M3.E4M3         (plain kind::f8f6f4)
    plus 8 instructions of scale-operand setup in the m=large instantiation (2688 vs 2696) --
    i.e. 0.3% of the instruction stream. MMA COUNT is 128 in both, LDS/LDG identical, so the
    2x measured speedup is NOT instruction count and NOT memory traffic. It is issue rate.

    instructions and still wins ~20%" survives as a fact and INVERTS as a conclusion: with the
